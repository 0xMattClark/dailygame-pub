# DailyGame.gg – Free Online Browser Games

**Discover, Play, and Enjoy Free Browser Games.**

[DailyGame.gg](https://dailygame.gg/) is an independent browser gaming platform dedicated to making casual online games accessible, enjoyable, and easy to play. No downloads or complicated setup required — just open your browser and start playing.

We currently feature two editions of the popular ocean-themed answer game, **Krillion**: Krillion Unlimited and our original Krillion Lite. Each offers a different way to challenge your knowledge, think beyond obvious answers, and explore the depths of the ocean.

🌐 **Website:** https://dailygame.gg/

## 🎮 Featured Games

DailyGame.gg currently features two Krillion editions, offering different gameplay experiences for players.

### 1. Krillion Unlimited

**Play more. Explore deeper. No daily limits.**

[Play Krillion Unlimited](https://dailygame.gg/)

Krillion Unlimited is a free browser-based trivia game provided by Daily Games / PlayDaily.org and embedded on DailyGame.gg.

Instead of choosing the most obvious answer, players are challenged to think of valid but less common answers. The rarer the accepted answer, the more Research Points (RP) they earn and the deeper their submarine dives.

**Key Features:**

- Seven open-ended questions per round.
- 25 seconds to answer each question.
- Rarity-based scoring rewards creative and uncommon answers.
- Unlimited replay — start another round whenever you want.
- No sign-up required to start playing.
- Multiplayer live rooms supporting up to 40 players.
- Private rooms with invitation codes or links.
- Ranked 1v1 matches for competitive play.

**Best for:** Players who enjoy quick trivia challenges, creative thinking, repeated practice, and competing with friends.

### 2. Krillion Lite

**Think what the crowd doesn't.**

[Play Krillion Lite](https://dailygame.gg/krillion-lite)

Krillion Lite is DailyGame.gg's original, self-developed ocean-themed answer game. Inspired by the appeal of rare-answer trivia, it introduces its own gameplay mechanics, scoring system, research progression, and specimen collection experience.

Players embark on an underwater research expedition, answering knowledge-based prompts with specific and uncommon answers. Every accepted answer generates research points and helps their submarine descend deeper.

**Key Features:**

- **Classic Mode:** Complete seven research prompts with upgrade opportunities.
- **Endless Mode:** Keep exploring with three air tanks and survive as long as possible.
- **Scan System:** Preview the rarity classification of valid answers before committing.
- **Current Upgrades:** Choose extra time, additional scans, or bonus depth.
- **Six Signal Classes:** From Common to Singular, with different research rewards.
- **Specimen Archive:** Discover and catalogue 24 marine species across five ocean zones.
- **Guest Profile:** Track personal statistics, achievements, and diving history.
- **Browser-Based Progress:** Personal records and discoveries are stored locally in your browser.

Krillion Lite uses its own built-in answer bank and independently curated rarity classifications. It does not require an external AI service or a user account.

**Best for:** Players who enjoy knowledge challenges, exploration, progression systems, collecting achievements, and endless replayability.

## ⚖️ Krillion Unlimited vs. Krillion Lite

Both games reward players for finding valid answers that are less obvious, but they offer different experiences.

| Feature           | Krillion Unlimited           | Krillion Lite                 |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Game Provider     | Daily Games / PlayDaily.org  | DailyGame.gg                  |
| Gameplay          | Open-ended trivia            | Ocean research adventure      |
| Replay            | Unlimited rounds             | Classic and Endless modes     |
| Questions         | 7 per round                  | Mode-dependent                |
| Answer Timer      | 25 seconds                   | 25 seconds                    |
| Scoring           | Answer rarity                | Six curated rarity classes    |
| Special Mechanics | Multiplayer and ranked 1v1   | Scans and current upgrades    |
| Progression       | Round-based results          | Depth and specimen collection |
| Personal Records  | Managed by game provider     | Stored locally in browser     |
| Account Required  | No account required to start | No account required           |

Choose **Krillion Unlimited** for unlimited trivia rounds and multiplayer competition, or **Krillion Lite** for a self-contained game with exploration, upgrades, achievements, and collection mechanics.
